About Us

Protecting You From Online Threats

Welcome to PhishGuard.AI, an AI-powered phishing and scam detection project designed to help users recognize potentially dangerous online content.

Cybercriminals use fake websites, fraudulent messages, malicious links, and social engineering techniques to steal personal information and financial data. PhishGuard.AI aims to help users evaluate suspicious content before interacting with it.

The system is intended to classify analyzed content as Safe, Suspicious, or Potentially Phishing/Scam, depending on the detection methods implemented by the application.
